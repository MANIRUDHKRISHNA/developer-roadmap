## 🧠 DSA System — Learn, Solve, Review

**Daily target:** 3–5 thoughtful problems. Quality and review beat streak-chasing.

| Order | Topic | Target | Progress |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Arrays, Strings, Hashing | 25 | ⬜ |
| 2 | Two Pointers, Sliding Window | 20 | ⬜ |
| 3 | Linked Lists, Stacks, Queues | 20 | ⬜ |
| 4 | Binary Search, Intervals | 20 | ⬜ |
| 5 | Recursion, Backtracking | 15 | ⬜ |
| 6 | Trees, BST, Heaps | 35 | ⬜ |
| 7 | Graphs | 30 | ⬜ |
| 8 | Dynamic Programming | 35 | ⬜ |

### Review loop

1. Solve without hints for 25–35 minutes.
2. Write the pattern, complexity, and mistake in an Issue or discussion.
3. Re-solve mistakes after **3 days, 7 days, and 21 days**.
4. Explain one solution aloud each week—as if in an interview.

## 💰 Money + MBA Plan

### First priority: financial runway

- [ ] Build a 3–6 month emergency fund
- [ ] Avoid lifestyle inflation after your first job
- [ ] Save automatically on payday
- [ ] Track spending and invest in your skills first

### MBA decision rule

An MBA is valuable when it clearly improves your **network, role options, leadership exposure, or access to capital**. Work 2–4 years first, then evaluate the return—not just the degree.

Learn business now: product thinking, customer interviews, finance basics, sales, negotiation, and leadership.
